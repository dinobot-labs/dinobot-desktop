# Code of Conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Constructive technical disagreement is welcome; harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and unwanted sexual attention are not. Respect privacy and do not publish others' personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and warn, restrict or exclude participants proportionately. Report concerns privately to dev0jsh@gmail.com with only the information needed to investigate. Reports will be handled discreetly, without promising absolute confidentiality.
